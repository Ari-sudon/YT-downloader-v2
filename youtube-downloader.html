About YT Glass

YT Glass is a client-side YouTube downloader with a liquid glass interface.

Features

  • Download videos up to 4K resolution
  • Extract audio as MP3, M4A, or OPUS
  • Support for Shorts, playlists, and Music
  • No server-side processing — runs entirely in browser

Note

Due to browser security (CORS), direct downloads may not work for all videos. For full functionality, use with a local proxy or backend service.
